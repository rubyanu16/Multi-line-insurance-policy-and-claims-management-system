# Multi-Line Insurance Policy and Claims Management System

A Salesforce-based application for managing insurance customers, policies, claims, and payments in a centralized platform.

## Features

*  Customer Management
*  Insurance Policy Management
*  Claims Management
*  Payment & Premium Tracking
*  Role-Based Access
*  Reports & Dashboards

## Insurance Types

* Vehicle Insurance
* Health Insurance
* Life Insurance
* Property Insurance

## Salesforce Technologies

* Salesforce Objects & Fields
* Lightning App
* Flows & Automation
* Validation Rules
* Reports & Dashboards
* Permission Sets & Security

## Objective

To streamline insurance policy and claims management using Salesforce automation and centralized data management.
